- **Island** — painted chart, counties, MegaBlocks, BWC DA downtown Silent City. Connecticut and Long Island Sound shores with real harbors. BBC DP / BBC DVDA and BWC DV marked on the north shore (open Shore). Ferry west around the island. Countess Island = The Gilded Grove.

- **City** — Silent City metro: BWC DA, south MegaBlock, Silent County gates, Route 69. Dashed pins are ST blanks (no street grid in the packet).
